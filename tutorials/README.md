## What changed from the inference-only tutorial

The previous notebook (`TASK-INFERENCE`) asked two prompts of the synthetic drawing and reported `not-measurable` because no metric helper existed. This notebook keeps that drawing and its two-prompt inference contract as Section 5 and adds the adaptation contract for one instruction (`Transcribe the handwritten text in this image.`) around it: a real labelled line set fetched with per-row-group pins, a corpus-level protocol in the carried `metrics.py` (uncapped micro and macro CER / WER, exact match, hypothesis length) with two non-adapted baselines, a bounded fine-tuning with the model's own objective on cached prefix hidden states, line panels, the two prompts re-run on the drawing after the shared decoder was tuned, and an artifact that reloads. The per-image `evaluation_report` helper is unchanged; `pipe.evaluate` is the corpus-level stage (`measured` / `measured-small-sample`). The pipeline now loads in float32 on every device (the earlier notebook used bfloat16 on CUDA), batches one prompt over many images with left padding, and admits text lines wider than 4,096 px (sides within 1..16,384 px, at most 4096² pixels).

## Conformance notes

- **Standalone carrier (ST1–ST8):** the primary path performs no clone, repository install, or repository import. Section 2 embeds the repository's three modules verbatim in topological dependency order (each tagged `metadata.dimer.embedded_module`; the only rewrites make `DEFAULT_WEIGHTS_DIR` working-directory-relative and remove internal relative package imports), Section 3 carries the 13-file snapshot manifest inline and asserts it against the module's constants before fetching, and `NOTEBOOK_SOURCE` records the repository, revision and module digests in the exports.
- **Parity (PAR1–PAR4):** `tests/test_notebook_parity.py` and `tools/validate_release_assets.py` fail when the carried modules, the inline manifest, or the inline pins differ from the repository, or when the notebook is not byte-identical to the generator's output.
- **Model acquisition (MOD1–MOD9, MOD12):** `stage_missing_files(WEIGHTS_DIR, allow_download=True)` fetches only the manifest entries a fresh runtime lacks, at the pinned revision (never `main`); `verify_snapshot` re-hashes every entry and asserts the pinned `model.safetensors` digest and byte count before the processor or the model is constructed; `SmolVLMPipeline.from_pretrained(weights_dir=WEIGHTS_DIR)` loads the verified files through the native classes (`local_files_only=True`, `trust_remote_code=False`) in float32 and picks CUDA when it is visible. No cell outside the carried modules calls `huggingface_hub`, `transformers`, `safetensors`, `pyarrow`, `urllib`, `torch.optim` or the model's processor directly.
- **Data (DAT1–DAT9, ST6):** the default sample is the Belfort-line dataset (`Teklia/Belfort-line` at the immutable parquet-conversion revision `c4a74bbd39f2df314752e7e6026649a39d365cbb`, MIT): the first eight row groups of `default/test/0000.parquet` read with HTTPS range requests in the carried `samples.py` (the shard's declared size and row count checked first, each row group refused unless its SHA-256 and byte total match `ROW_GROUP_PINS`; about 44 MB), and `read_corpus` makes each row a record with its 128-px line image and its transcript (whitespace runs collapsed); `build_sample_dataset(seed=SPLIT_SEED)` draws 600 / 60 / 140 and `check_split_disjoint` asserts no image (by decoded-pixel digest) is shared. The template adds a second *External access* bullet naming the host, the revision, the byte count and the licence.
- **Stages (§4.1):** `fetch_corpus` + `read_corpus` + `build_sample_dataset` / `load_byod_dataset` + `split_dataset` (sample acquisition) → `validate_dataset` per split + `check_split_disjoint` + refusal probes (validation, with three digests) → `validate_inputs` per prompt + the over-long-prompt refusal probe + `generate` for two prompts with structural checks + `evaluation_report` on the drawing (inference contract) → `empty_baseline` + `constant_baseline` + `pipe.evaluate` on the frozen transcription instruction with four hypotheses printed (baselines) → `pipe.adapt` (bounded fine-tuning) → `pipe.evaluate` on validation and test with the comparison (evaluation) → the line panels + the two prompts on the drawing again (inference) → `pipe.save_artifact` → `SmolVLMPipeline.from_artifact` with the parity assertion (export and reload).
- **Score semantics (UNC1–UNC4):** captions, answers and transcripts are generated text with no score, and the notebook says so before the first call; the corpus rates are uncapped Levenshtein rates over one crowdsourced transcription at stated normalisations, with the hypothesis length beside them so a rate above 1.0 is read as over-generation; no acceptance threshold ships. The drawing asked again after adaptation carries a `not-measurable` verdict for both prompts (open-ended answers, no reference), and the notebook names the missing dispersion.
- **Baselines and evidence (EVAL6/EVAL10/EVAL11):** the empty string, the constant transcript and the frozen model are scored on the same 140 lines as the adapted model, on the same rates; every metric is labelled as one seeded split of one sample with no dispersion estimate, and the notebook says explicitly where the frozen model sits against the empty baseline (build record: the frozen model does not transcribe the lines: it answers in English about the handwriting, invents dates and sentences, and loops on one word until the 128-token budget — 14 of 140 generations truncated and 10,515 hypothesis characters for 6,169 reference characters, so the CER passes 1.0 by over-generation rather than silence) and that the gain is measured on one French council's cursive only.
- **Split integrity (SPL1–SPL8):** the split is seeded and image-disjoint; validation (60 lines) selects the epoch on CER and the test split is used for nothing but the final evaluation; the notebook states that lines cut from the same page share a hand and must be split by page, writer or volume.
- **Ceilings (VAL6):** `MIN_IMAGE_SIDE` (1), `MAX_IMAGE_SIDE` (16384), `MAX_IMAGE_PIXELS` (4096²), `MAX_NEW_TOKENS` (512), `DEFAULT_MAX_NEW_TOKENS` (128), `DEFAULT_LINE_MAX_NEW_TOKENS` (128), `MAX_TEXT_CHARS` (2,000 prompt characters), `MAX_IMAGES` (1), `DECODING` (`greedy`), `TRANSCRIBE_PROMPT`, the record bounds (8..5,000), the transcript bound (512) and the id pattern are stated in the Prerequisites and printed before the model runs; four dataset refusals and one over-long-prompt refusal are demonstrated.
- **BYOD (DAT10–DAT19):** `USE_BYOD` defaults to `False` so the sample path never opens an upload dialog; when enabled, one zip is read member by member (names flattened, no `extractall`) or a directory walked, each row of `transcripts.csv` becomes a record, and the records flow through the same validation, image-disjoint split, baselines, fine-tuning, evaluation, export and reload cells. The expected layout and the privacy guidance are stated in the Prerequisites and in Section 4, and the training split's summary is written to `outputs/smolvlm_vision_language_train.csv`.
- Licence: the weights are Apache-2.0 and the notebook, README, card and `docs/WEIGHTS.md` say so; Belfort-line is MIT and is not redistributed — the export records the corpus name, repository, revision, file, licence, language and row groups under `corpus`.
- `tools/validate_release_assets.py` performs source validation only. It does not satisfy the clean-runtime execution requirement; a release review must confirm that a recorded clean run in `../docs/release-verification.md` matches the notebook revision under review before the status is promoted to `Release-grade`.

## Outputs

The default notebook path writes (under `outputs/`):

- `smolvlm_vision_language_train.csv` — the training split's summary table (id, file name, image size, character and word counts, transcript, provenance)
- `smolvlm_vision_language_example_line.png` — the first training line
- `smolvlm_vision_language_input_manifest.json` — the combined inference-contract manifest (schema, the two prompt requests, one recorded rejection finding)
- `smolvlm_vision_language_drawing_frozen.json`, `smolvlm_vision_language_drawing_adapted.json` — the two prompts' answers, timings, structural checks and `evaluation_report` before and after adaptation
- `smolvlm_vision_language_evaluation_report.json` — baselines, frozen and adapted transcription metrics on validation and test, the per-line rows with both hypotheses, the comparison, the adaptation history
- `smolvlm_vision_language_examples/` — six held-out lines as panels (the line image with the reference, the frozen transcript and the adapted transcript)
- `smolvlm_vision_language_adapter/` — `adapter.safetensors` + `manifest.json`
- `smolvlm_vision_language_result.json` — `NOTEBOOK_SOURCE`, model identity, snapshot, corpus, the two prompts before and after adaptation with their reports, comparison, artifact digest, reload parity, runtime
